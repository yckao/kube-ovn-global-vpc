# Code of conduct

Participate respectfully and welcome people with different backgrounds and
experience. Critique ideas and code with evidence; leave room for correction.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption,
and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable. These
expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews and representation.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation or exclude people
when necessary. They should consider context, avoid retaliation and explain
moderation decisions when that does not expose private details.

Report conduct concerns through a private channel listed on the maintainer's
GitHub profile. If none is available, request a private contact in an issue
without identifying affected people or describing sensitive details. Reports
are handled with discretion on a best-effort basis. A dedicated community
reporting contact remains an open governance improvement.
